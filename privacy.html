Privacy policy

Last updated September 29, 2026 · Beta version

AlleGrow is in beta and is currently run by an independent developer. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the apps do with information. If anything is unclear, ask us.

The short version

  • The practice timer never records. AlleGrow listens through the microphone to time practice, but the sound is analyzed on the phone, tablet or computer itself. It is never recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • Recordings only when a parent allows them. If a parent turns it on, a child can record a short clip of an assignment and send it to their teacher. Nothing is recorded until they press Record, and nothing is sent until they press Send.
  • We collect as little as we can. A first name or nickname for each child, practice numbers (minutes, days, scores and badges), and – only if a parent or teacher adds one – a small photo.
  • Parents are in control. Practice stays on your device unless you turn on sync between your own devices or choose to connect your child to their teacher.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling data. We don’t use advertising or analytics services, and we never sell or rent information.

Who AlleGrow is for

AlleGrow has two parts: the family app, used by children to time their music practice under a parent’s supervision, and AlleGrow Studio, used by music teachers. Children don’t create accounts and aren’t asked for contact details. Parents set up the family app (protected by a parent PIN) and decide what is shared.

Children’s privacy

AlleGrow is designed to be used by children, including children under 13, with a parent or teacher. We follow these rules:

  • We don’t ask children for personal information and we don’t let children make public posts or contact other people.
  • A parent must agree, in the app, before a child’s practice is shared with a teacher. The date of that agreement is saved on the student’s record.
  • When a teacher adds a student to AlleGrow Studio, the teacher confirms they have the family’s permission to use the student’s name for lessons (see the terms).
  • Photos are optional. Only a parent can add a child’s photo in the family app (behind the parent PIN). It is shrunk to a small picture on the device before it’s saved, is shared only with a teacher the parent connects, and can be removed any time in Parent settings. Teachers can add a small photo to help recognize a student only with the family’s permission.
  • Recordings are off until a parent turns them on in Parent settings (behind the parent PIN), for each child. A parent or the teacher can delete a recording, and disconnecting from a teacher can remove them all.
  • Parents can see everything the family app stores about their child in the app itself, and can delete it at any time (see “Deleting information” below). You can also ask us to review or delete your child’s information, or to stop any further use of it.

How we use information

Only to run AlleGrow: to show practice and progress, share it with a connected teacher, show teachers’ assignments and lesson times to families, keep calendars up to date, send teachers the emails they turn on, and improve AlleGrow using feedback you send us. We don’t use information to advertise to anyone.

Services that help run AlleGrow

  • Google Firebase – the database, and sign-in for teachers and parents. If you choose to sign in with Google, Microsoft or Yahoo, that company confirms who you are and shares your name and email with us.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare – host the apps and the calendar and email service.
  • Resend – delivers the emails teachers turn on.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and Google’s MediaPipe – deliver fonts, app code and the sound-recognition model, which then runs on your device.

Like any website, these services see technical information such as your device’s IP address when the app loads. They process information only to provide their service to us.

Deleting information

  • Families: remove a player in Parent settings to delete their practice history (from this device and, with sync, from our database). “Disconnect” (or “Switch teacher”) stops sharing with a teacher, and can also remove the recordings and practice history, badges and photo already shared with them. A parent account can be removed in Settings → Devices & sync.
  • Teachers: archive or delete a student in AlleGrow Studio. Deleting a student removes their record and disconnects the family.
  • Anyone: contact us and we’ll delete information we hold about you or your child.

Keeping information safe

Family, student and calendar records can only be opened with long random codes, and each teacher’s studio can only be opened by that teacher when signed in. We keep the information we collect to a minimum. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep family codes and invite links private.

Changes

If we make meaningful changes – especially to anything involving children’s information – we’ll update this page and let families and teachers know in the app before the change takes effect.
