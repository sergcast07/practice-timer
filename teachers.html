Sound familiar?

You see each student for half an hour a week. Everything that decides how they progress happens in the other 167 hours.

“Did you practice?”
“Yes!”

Practice logs are filled in the night before the lesson – if at all. You can hear the truth in the first minute, but by then the lesson has started.

“What were we working on?”

The first ten minutes go on catching up: which piece, which tempo, what happened this week. That’s a third of a lesson.

By Wednesday, it’s fading.

Motivation is highest right after the lesson. Without a nudge during the week, practice drifts – and you only find out six days later.

Teachers ask

Do my families have to pay or sign up for anything?

No. The family app is free, runs in any modern browser, and children don’t need accounts. A parent just opens your invite link and agrees to share.

What if a student doesn’t use the app?

You can still manage their lessons, assignments, notes, performances and reports in your studio. Families can also log practice by hand, and it’s shown as “logged by hand” so you know.

How does it know they’re really practicing?

The app listens through the device’s microphone and uses a small sound-recognition model that runs on the device itself. The timer counts music, not talking or silence. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere – only the minutes and scores.

I teach young beginners. Won’t scores discourage them?

You decide. Tuning, focus and rhythm scores can be shown as numbers, as gentle trends, or turned off – for your whole studio or just one student. Parents can also turn tuning off for their child.

Which instruments does it work with?

Any instrument, including voice. Tuning scores are only used where the player shapes every note (strings, voice, winds and brass) – never for piano, guitar, ukulele or drums.

Can I bring my existing student list?

Yes. Paste it straight from Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers, or upload a CSV file. Allegrow checks it before adding anyone and gives you every family’s invite link in one place.

Does it work with my calendar?

Yes. Subscribe once in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook and your lessons, changes and performances stay up to date on their own.

What happens after the beta?

We’ll introduce paid plans for larger studios, with plenty of notice. Beta partners will be looked after. The family app will stay free.
